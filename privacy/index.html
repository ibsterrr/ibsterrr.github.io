Lotl privacy policy

Test version. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Lotl is a virtual pet that reflects how you are living, and can help you with goals you choose, such as drinking less alcohol, less sugar or caffeine, smoking less, or sleeping and moving more. This policy explains what Lotl uses and where it goes. The short version: everything stays on your iPhone.

What Lotl uses

  • What Lotl remembers, if you leave it on. A few short notes from what you say, so Lotl can ask about them later: what would make today great, and plans you mention (for example "the dentist"). Notes about drinking, health, money or other sensitive things are not kept. Notes are kept for two weeks.
  • Lotl's journey. Once a week Lotl keeps a picture of itself and the pond, with a line on what changed.
  • Your goals and how each day went. The goals you pick (up to three, for example alcohol, sugar and sweets, energy drinks and caffeine, takeaway food, smoking or vaping, scrolling, sleep, moving, mindfulness, drinking water or eating well). For a goal you are cutting down on, one answer a day: none, low, medium or high, and any "swaps" you note (for example an alcohol-free drink or a healthy snack). Lotl does not count individual drinks, cigarettes or anything else.
  • Sit with me. When you use it, Lotl notes that you did and, if you answer, whether the hard moment passed. The goal is never named in its notifications.
  • What you tell Lotl. When you talk or type about your day, your words are turned into text and read by an AI model on your iPhone to understand any drinks, how each goal went, how you feel, and things like gratitude, kindness, achievements and your own habits. That changes how Lotl and the pond look.
  • Apple Health, if you allow it. Steps, exercise minutes, workouts, active energy, sleep, mindful minutes, State of Mind, and water and food energy if you already record them. Lotl also writes to Apple Health: the time you spend breathing with Lotl (as mindful minutes), and how you say each day felt (as State of Mind). Lotl does not write anything about drinking, smoking or your other goals to Apple Health. You choose each of these in Apple's permission screen and can change them at any time in the Health app.
  • The weather where you are, only if you switch it on. Lotl asks your iPhone for your rough location and uses it to fetch the current weather, so the pond can match the sky. See "Where it goes" below.
  • How you use the app. Which days you open it, your weekly goals, and Lotl's own progress.
  • How Lotl and the pond look. Lotl's colours, markings and pond are worked out on your iPhone from the things above. They are a picture of your habits, so anyone you show your phone to can see them.

Where it goes

  • It stays on your iPhone. Lotl has no accounts and no server. Nothing you log, say or share from Apple Health is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • One exception, and only if you switch on "Match the weather outside": your location, rounded to about ten kilometres, is sent to a weather service to get the current weather. Nothing else is sent with it: no name, no account, nothing about your health or your goals. Lotl does not store your location. You can switch this off in Settings at any time. In this test version the weather service is Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). A later version will use Apple Weather.
  • Speech and AI run on your iPhone. Your voice is turned into text by Apple's on-device speech system, and the text is read by Apple's on-device model. Audio is not recorded or kept. The same on-device model writes Lotl's short replies to what you say; they are not advice.
  • Apple Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared with anyone.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and we do not track you.
  • Friends. If you make friends in Lotl, your Lotl's name and how it looks (its colours, pattern, size, what it is wearing, and simple touches like sleepy eyes or a headband after exercise) are stored in Apple's iCloud so your friends' phones can show it. So are your friend code, a random id for your Lotl, friend requests, and things you send a friend (hang-out invitations, treats, gifts, eggs, things you lend their Lotl, pearls their Lotl won in your pond, and short notes such as "Pip is looking after Sam", which use only your Lotls' names and can appear as a notification on your friend's phone). How your friend's Lotl looks includes a few hints from their habits, such as sleepy eyes after a short night or flushed gills when they have had no calm moments this week; no figures are shared. Nothing else goes there: never your goals, drinks, health readings, scores, what you tell Lotl, your name, email or contacts. Anyone who has your friend code can see your Lotl's name and look. You can remove a friend at any time, make a new code, or choose Leave friends, which deletes everything your phone put in iCloud for friends. Friends need you to be signed in to iCloud.
  • Feedback in test versions. Test versions of Lotl have a feedback button. What you say is turned into text on your phone and put into an email that you can read and edit; it is only sent if you tap Send in your own email app. Lotl itself does not send it anywhere.
  • Test versions. If you installed Lotl through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us. That is Apple's service, covered by Apple's own terms.

Keeping and deleting

  • The text of what you tell Lotl is kept on your iPhone so you can delete it. In Lotl, open Settings and choose "Delete everything I've said". This also deletes what Lotl remembers. Each remembered note can also be deleted on its own, or remembering switched off, under "What Lotl remembers" in Settings.
  • Saving a copy of Lotl. If you choose "Save a copy", Lotl writes one file with Lotl, your days (including your goals and drinks) and your settings, and you decide where it goes (for example iCloud Drive). What you said aloud, the notes Lotl remembers and the journey pictures are not in it. Keep it somewhere private.
  • Deleting the app removes everything Lotl has stored. Mindful minutes and State of Mind entries Lotl wrote to Apple Health stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.
  • If you back up your iPhone, Lotl's data is included in your backup under your own Apple account.

Health and safety

Lotl is not a medical app and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If drinking, smoking or anything else is starting to feel hard to control, please talk to a doctor or a local support service.

Children

Lotl is for adults. It is not intended for children.
