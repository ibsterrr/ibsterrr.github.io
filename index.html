How it works

Your day shows up in the pond

No forms and no telling-off. Just a little life that reflects how you're looking after yourself.

01

Live your day

Choose what you'd like to work on: less alcohol or sugar, better sleep, more movement, calm. Lotl reads your steps and sleep from Apple Health.

02

Watch your pond

Good days clear the water and grow new plants overnight. Lotl grows up, earns colours and markings, and moves into new homes.

03

Tell Lotl about your day

Talk to Lotl in the evening. It listens, answers back and remembers what matters to you.

Grows with you

From a bare pond to a magical one

It starts as an egg in an almost empty pond. Every good day adds something you can see.

  • Clear water, plants, coral and visitors that come and go
  • Colours and markings that come from how you sleep, move and stay calm
  • No two Lotls end up alike, because no two lives are
Better together

Friends' Lotls come to visit

Invite a friend and your Lotls can visit each other's ponds.

  • Play together: race for pearls and chase the ball
  • Look after each other: lend a tired friend your night mask
  • Sleepovers: visitors can stay the night
Your day at a glance

See how today is going

Rings for moving, sleep, calm, mood and your goals fill as the day goes, and the pond lights up with them.

  • Flower buds open by Lotl's home as you meet your goals
  • A golden hour when every ring is strong
  • A gentle week summary, never a telling-off
